## Featured Projects

### 1. Retail Sales Performance Analysis

A Python-based analysis of retail transaction data covering product performance, category and regional sales, promotions, sales trends, and shipment timing.

**Tools:** Python, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

**View the project:**  
[Retail Sales Performance Analysis](https://github.com/abrahamoluwatobiloba/retail-sales-performance-analysis)

**Key capabilities demonstrated:**

- Data-quality assessment.
- Missing-value treatment.
- Date and numeric-field preparation.
- Revenue and unit analysis.
- Product, category, and regional comparisons.
- Promotion analysis.
- Time-series analysis.
- Order-to-ship analysis.
- Business recommendations and limitations.
